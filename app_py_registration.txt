ADD THIS TO app.py
=================

Find the section where `app = Flask(__name__)` and your `ask_ai` function are
defined. After both exist, add:

    from lesson_plan_routes import init_lesson_builder
    init_lesson_builder(app, ask_ai)

Do not register the same blueprint twice.

This package adds a new blueprint named `lesson_builder`, with:
  GET  /lesson-plan
  POST /lesson-plan/generate
  POST /lesson-plan/download

If your existing app already has a different route at `/lesson-plan`, remove
or disable that old route before registering this one to avoid a URL conflict.
Do not replace app.py wholesale; preserve your login, chat and other routes.
